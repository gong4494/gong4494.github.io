The trip

Every summer, teams of 12-and-under players from across the country spend a week in Cooperstown, New York, just down the road from the National Baseball Hall of Fame. Players live together in the player barracks, trade pins with kids from every state, and play games on fields built for this one week of their childhood.

Will's team plays August 19–25, 2027.
